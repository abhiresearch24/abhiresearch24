## Hi, I'm Abhishek Srivastava 👋

Project Associate, Department of Mechanical Engineering, **IIT Palakkad** · Ph.D., **IIT Delhi**

I work at the intersection of **scientific machine learning and uncertainty quantification** for thermal-fluid systems:

- **Bayesian inverse problems** — variational inference and VAE-based frameworks for estimating unknown parameters and fields with calibrated uncertainty
- **Identifiability and optimal experimental design** — Fisher-information analysis and sensor placement for inverse heat transfer (jet impingement cooling)
- **Physics-constrained ML and surrogate modelling** — fast, interpretable surrogates of multiphysics simulations for design optimisation
- **Applications** — electrostatic particle collection, heat transfer, fluid and solid mechanics
